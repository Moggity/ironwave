# Beginner app changelog

## Package 1: scaffold
- Skeleton for the beginner app: bare `index.html`, empty `BEGINNER_CONFIG`, `BEGINNER_EXERCISES`, `Coach`, `Store`, `UI`, test harnesses, `scripts/assemble-beginner.js`, and a `beginner` CI job on Node 18 and 20.

## Package 2: exercise data
- 41 beginner exercises with equipment keys, form cues and placeholder media, plus the five goals (Bigger arms, Bigger chest, Legs and glutes, Look fit, Look strong) with `beginnerGoalFocus`, where a skipped goal means Look fit.
